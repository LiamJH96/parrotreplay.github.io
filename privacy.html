Privacy Policy

Version 1.2.0  ·  Effective Date: 20 November 2026  ·  Parrot Replay Software

Contact: support@parrotreplay.com

01 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Parrot Replay Software ("we", "us") collects and uses personal information through Parrot Replay (the "Software"), its online licensing system, support services and website. It also explains how we identify possible misuse of free trials and how long information is kept. Please read this notice alongside the End-User License Agreement (EULA).

02 Information We Collect

Licensing and account information: licence keys, licence type, duration, activation and expiry dates, licence status, email address where supplied, and records of activation, validation, release and revocation.

Device and technical information: hardware identifiers associated with a device; Windows version and build; computer and motherboard manufacturer and model; device name and Windows username; application version; microphone, speaker and headphone device names; relevant audio-driver installation information; IP address; and timestamps. We may retain the current technical profile, an initial device profile and records of changes to these details. A hardware identifier, even if hashed, may be personal information when it can be linked to a person or device.

Trial and security information: dates of trial requests and expiry, previous trial use, pseudonymised device fingerprints, technical similarities between trial requests, and administrative notes or case records created when possible trial misuse is investigated.

Transactions and creator programmes: Stripe payment references, purchase amounts, coupon or creator codes, and, where relevant, creator contact details, commissions and payout details such as bank account and sort code. Payment-card information is processed by Stripe rather than stored by Parrot Replay.

Website information: analytics and cookie information described in sections 14 and 15, and technical request information handled by our hosting and security providers.

03 How Information Is Collected

Information may be collected when you purchase, activate, validate or release a licence; request or use a free trial; use the Software while online; contact support; participate in a creator programme; or use our website. Some technical information is supplied automatically by the Software or recorded by our servers.

04 Purposes and Lawful Bases

We use information to supply and administer licences and trials, send purchase and support communications, verify licence status, process payments, and provide the services requested. Where necessary, this processing supports performance of a contract with you.

We also use technical, activity and security information to protect our licensing system, detect possible repeated free trials, investigate suspected misuse, respond to support requests, maintain service security and reliability, understand aggregate service usage, and assess technical compatibility trends to improve the Software. We rely on our legitimate interests in operating and protecting the service, balanced against the privacy interests of affected users.

We process records where required to comply with applicable legal obligations, including relevant financial and accounting requirements. Non-essential website analytics cookies are used only where the required consent has been given.

05 Free Trial Eligibility and Abuse Review

A free trial is intended to be used once per eligible device unless we expressly authorise another trial. To enforce this, we may compare device identifiers, technical profiles, IP addresses, trial dates and changes in technical information across trial requests.

The system may flag combinations of activity that suggest repeated trial use. A flag or suspicion score is an investigative aid, not proof of wrongdoing. Suspected cases are reviewed by an authorised administrator; a suspicion score alone does not automatically revoke a paid licence or determine that a person has committed abuse. Historical IP information known to be unreliable is excluded from the relevant comparison process.

To remember prior trial use after a trial licence is deleted, we retain a pseudonymised device fingerprint produced using a keyed cryptographic hash. This is not the original hardware identifier, but it remains personal information where it can be linked to a device. Authorised administrators can explicitly permit another trial.

06 Third-Party Services and Storage

We use Stripe for payments, Resend for transactional emails, Railway for backend hosting and databases, and Cloudflare for website delivery and security. Google Analytics is used for website analytics as described below. GitHub is used for Software update checks and installer downloads. When the Software connects to GitHub for these purposes, GitHub or its delivery providers may receive the connecting IP address and standard request information, including the time of the request, the requested resource and the user agent. These providers may process information on our behalf or under their own privacy notices, as appropriate.

Data may be processed or stored outside the UK. Where international transfers of personal information occur, we will use an applicable lawful transfer mechanism or safeguard where required.

07 Retention and Deletion

Historical technical changes, eligible activity and IP logs, and closed investigative records are subject to a general 365-day retention policy. Current technical information and an initial hardware profile may be retained while a paid licence remains active and are not necessarily deleted on the same schedule. Technical information associated with an expired licence is ordinarily removed after the applicable post-expiry retention period, subject to the protections below.

Minimal pseudonymised trial-eligibility records are retained for up to 1,095 days (approximately three years) after the associated trial expires, including where the original trial licence has been deleted. Expired trial licence records may separately be removed under the existing 180-day cleanup process.

Information needed for an open investigation, dispute, security incident or legal obligation may be kept longer while that need continues. Licence, purchase, invoice and accounting records follow separate retention requirements and are not necessarily deleted by the technical-data cleanup. Some older records may also remain in recovery backups until those backups expire or are deleted; backup copies are not necessarily removed by a live-database cleanup.

08 Financial and Creator Data

Where you participate in a creator or affiliate programme, we use contact, commission and payout details to calculate and issue payments and keep appropriate business records. We do not publish bank details. Please keep payout information accurate.

09 Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including restricted administrative access and multi-factor authentication. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

10 Audio Recordings

Audio recordings made with Parrot Replay remain on your device. We do not receive, collect or store the content of your voice recordings through the licensing service.

11 Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal information, restrict or object to certain processing (including processing based on legitimate interests), and receive certain information in a portable format. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). Contact support@parrotreplay.com to make a request. Some information may need to be retained where an applicable legal basis or exception permits this.

12 Children's Privacy

The Software is not intended for children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child contrary to applicable requirements, we will take appropriate action.

13 Changes to This Policy

We may update this notice to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The effective date and version above identify the current notice. Where required, we will give appropriate notice of material changes.

14 Website Usage and Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics to help us understand visits, pages viewed, general location and device categories. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies. Non-essential analytics are activated only after the relevant consent is provided through the website's cookie controls. See https://policies.google.com/privacy.

15 Cookies

The website uses essential cookies where necessary for operation and optional analytics cookies where consent is given. You can accept or reject non-essential cookies and change your preferences using the website's cookie controls.

16 Infrastructure and Hosting

Cloudflare helps deliver and protect our website and may process IP addresses and request metadata for security and reliability. See https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/.

17 Contact and Summary

For privacy enquiries, contact support@parrotreplay.com. Parrot Replay collects licensing and technical information to operate and secure its services, including checks against repeat free trials. Suspected abuse is subject to human review. Audio recordings stay local to your device.
